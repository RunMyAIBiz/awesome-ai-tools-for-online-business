## AI Assistants and Research

- [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) - OpenAI's all-purpose assistant for brainstorming, writing and analysis. 🆓
- [Claude](https://claude.com/) - Anthropic's assistant for writing, analysis and long documents. 🆓
- [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/) - AI answer engine that cites its sources, handy for market research. 🆓
- [Manus](https://manus.im/) - General-purpose AI agent that runs multi-step research, slide and website tasks. 🆓
- [Genspark](https://www.genspark.ai/) - AI "super agent" workspace with agents for slides, docs and sheets. See site for plans.
- [DisconfirmAI](https://disconfirmai.com/en) - AI investment research platform that looks for disconfirming evidence before you form a conviction. 🆓

## AI App and Website Builders

- [Lovable](https://lovable.dev/) - Describe an app or site in plain English and it builds it, with hosting and a backend. 🆓
- [Bolt.new](https://bolt.new/) - Browser-based AI builder for websites and web apps, with hosting included. 🆓
- [Replit](https://replit.com/) - AI agent that builds and deploys apps from a description. Paid plans from $20/mo, with usage-based AI pricing. 💲 (see site for any free option)
- [Base44](https://base44.com/) - No-code AI app builder with a built-in database, login and analytics. 🆓
- [v0](https://v0.app/) - Vercel's AI builder that generates web interfaces and apps from prompts. 🆓

## AI Coding Assistants

*For people comfortable working with code and project files.*

- [Claude Code](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/overview) - Anthropic's coding agent that reads, edits and runs code in your project. Needs a paid Claude plan. 💲
- [GitHub Copilot](https://github.com/features/copilot) - AI pair programmer inside your code editor. 🆓
- [Cursor](https://cursor.com/) - AI-first code editor. 🆓

## Websites, Hosting and Stores

- [WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/) - Free, open-source website software. You supply the hosting. 🆓
- [Wix](https://www.wix.com/) - Drag-and-drop website builder with AI site tools. 🆓
- [Hostinger](https://www.hostinger.com/) - Budget web hosting and domains. 💲
- [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/) - Hosted e-commerce store builder. 💲 (free trial)

## Design, Slides and Visuals

- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) - Design tool with AI features for social posts, slides, PDFs and video. 🆓
- [Gamma](https://gamma.app/) - AI-generated presentations, documents and one-page websites. 🆓

## Email and Audience

- [Kit](https://kit.com/) - Email marketing for creators. The free plan covers your list, and paid plans add automations and sequences. 🆓
- [Resend](https://resend.com/) - Developer-friendly email-sending API, often used with AI-built apps. 🆓

## Selling and Payments

- [Stripe](https://stripe.com/) - Card payments and checkout. No monthly fee, priced per transaction. 🧾
- [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/) - Sell digital products with no monthly fee. Takes a percentage of each sale. 🧾
- [Payhip](https://payhip.com/) - Digital product and course storefront. Free plan with a transaction fee. 🆓
- [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/) - Marketplace for handmade and digital goods. Listing and transaction fees, and AI use must be disclosed. 🧾
- [Amazon KDP](https://kdp.amazon.com/) - Self-publish ebooks and paperbacks on Amazon. Free to publish, royalty-based. 🧾

## Automation

- [Zapier](https://zapier.com/) - Connect apps and automate workflows, with AI steps and agents. 🆓
- [Make](https://www.make.com/) - Visual automation builder, priced by credits. 🆓
- [n8n](https://n8n.io/) - Workflow automation you can self-host, or use their cloud version. 🆓 (self-hosted)
- [IFTTT](https://ifttt.com/) - Simple "if this, then that" automations between apps and services. 🆓

## Forms and Scheduling

- [Tally](https://tally.so/) - Form builder with a generous free plan. 🆓
- [Calendly](https://calendly.com/) - Booking links for calls and appointments. 🆓

## Contributing

Suggestions are welcome! Please open an issue or a pull request that:

1. Adds **one tool per pull request**, in the right category, in the format `[Name](official-link) - One-line description. <legend icon>`.
2. Uses the tool's **official website** only. No referral, affiliate or tracking links.
3. Is genuinely useful for **starting or running an online business**, not just a general AI tool.
4. Uses a neutral, factual description, without hype or income claims.

Dead links and outdated pricing notes get removed or fixed during regular link checks.
